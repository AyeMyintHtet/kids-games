# Privacy Policy for Learny Land (Kids Games)

Effective date: February 27, 2026

## 1. Overview

Learny Land is a kids educational game app. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your choices.

## 2. Data We Collect

We collect and store limited in-app data to provide gameplay features:

- Game progress (levels, scores, streaks, achievements)
- App settings (language, sound/music preferences)
- Optional child profile values entered in the app (nickname and avatar/gender choice)

This app is designed not to collect sensitive personal information such as real names, addresses, phone numbers, or precise location.

## 3. How Data Is Used

We use data only to:

- Save progress locally on the device
- Restore sessions and settings
- Show game-related summaries and achievements
- Improve gameplay stability and performance

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We do not share personal data with advertising networks from within the app.

Platform providers (for example Google Play or Apple App Store) may process technical data as part of app delivery and distribution under their own policies.

## 5. Microphone Permission

The app may request microphone permission for audio-related features.

If microphone access is used, audio is intended only for the relevant in-app feature and is not sold to third parties.

## 6. Data Storage and Retention

- Gameplay and settings data are primarily stored on-device.
- You can clear app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from device settings.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

This app is intended for children and families. We aim to follow child privacy requirements (including COPPA-style principles) by minimizing data collection and avoiding targeted advertising.

Parents or guardians can contact us to request deletion support guidance.

## 8. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards for locally stored app data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above will be updated when changes are made.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us at:

- GitHub Issues: https://github.com/AyeMyintHtet/kids-games/issues
